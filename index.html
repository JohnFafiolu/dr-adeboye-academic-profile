About the scholar

Statistics for understanding complex systems and informing decisions.

Dr. Nureni Olawale Adeboye is a Statistician and Data Scientist, and the Head of the Department of Statistics at Osun State University, Osogbo, Nigeria. He holds National Diploma and Higher National Diploma qualifications in Statistics, a Bachelor of Science degree in Statistics, a Master of Science degree in Statistics, a Postgraduate Diploma in Planning, Research and Statistics, and a PhD in Statistics.

His professional experience spans banking operations, university and polytechnic teaching, research supervision, academic administration, statistical consulting and editorial service. He has taught at undergraduate, postgraduate and professional levels and has contributed to research projects and scholarly discussions in Nigeria and internationally.

His research interests include panel data econometrics, time-series econometrics, official statistics, operations research, machine learning, bioinformatics and geospatial analysis.

Research profile

Research interests & current work

His research engages statistical theory and applied methods to investigate economic, social, health and environmental questions.

∑

Panel Data Econometrics

Dynamic panel models, heterogeneity, heteroscedasticity, periodicity and econometric approaches to economic and institutional data.

⌁

Time-Series Econometrics

Forecasting, long-memory processes, volatility modelling, ARIMA-family methods and the analysis of temporal patterns.

▦

Official Statistics

Statistical systems, data production and use, big data, statistical capacity and evidence for public policy.

◇

Machine Learning & Data Science

Predictive modelling, classification, data-driven methods and the application of machine learning to real-world problems.

⌖

Geospatial & Environmental Statistics

Spatial and spatio-temporal analysis of health, ecological and environmental risk patterns.

＋

Biostatistics & Survival Analysis

Statistical modelling of disease patterns, survival outcomes and public-health questions.
